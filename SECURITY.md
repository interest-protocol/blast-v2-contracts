# Security policy

## Report a vulnerability

Email **[jose@blast.fun](mailto:jose@blast.fun)**. Jose is Blast's security
contact and owns the response to vulnerability reports.

We acknowledge reports within **four business hours**, Monday–Friday,
09:00–17:00 **Europe/Lisbon**. Time outside these hours counts from the next
business opening. This is an initial response target; remediation depends on
the severity and scope of the issue.

This channel covers blast.fun, docs.blast.fun, Blast-operated APIs, and Blast's
application, SDK, and smart contracts. Include the affected URL, repository or
package version, reproduction steps, expected and actual behavior, potential
impact, and a minimal proof of concept when available.

Keep vulnerability details private while we investigate and coordinate public
disclosure with you. Do not open public GitHub issues containing exploit
details. Avoid including passwords, private keys, access tokens, or other
people's personal data. Use accounts and assets you control, minimize testing,
and stop if testing could expose data, disrupt services, or put funds at risk.

The public [disclosure policy](https://docs.blast.fun/security/reporting/) and
[security.txt](https://blast.fun/.well-known/security.txt) provide the same
contact. Keep this policy and security.txt current when the contact or response
hours change, and renew security.txt before its expiry date.
