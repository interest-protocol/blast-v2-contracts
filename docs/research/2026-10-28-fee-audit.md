Source audit of `blast-v2-contracts` at `213d53c` (main). Every source module in
`sui/launchpad`, `sui/migrators`, and `sui/presale` was read, together with the
partner payout implementation and the pinned vesting source. No Move code,
policy values, or production state was changed. This report separates source
initializers, dated on-chain policy, and settings snapshotted into each launch.

## Assessment

The curve's fee burden is high: with the dated 1.5% on each leg, a trade retains
`0.985 * 0.985 = 0.970225` of its value in the infinitesimal, no-burn,
no-rounding limit. That is approximately **2.9775% per trade before price impact**,
not 1.5%. At the dated split, about 1.935375% of notional goes to Blast and
1.042125% to creator fees in that same reference limit. Token-denominated fees
and finite curve price movement prevent treating these approximations as an
exact quote for a large trade. A higher optional sell burn adds further loss.
My assessment: the combined curve fee should be reduced or justified with a
clear product benefit; displaying only one 1.5% rate is inadequate.

The 1.5% graduation Quote charge is modest by itself, but the 2.5% supply grant
adds a separate token allocation on top of recurring curve and LP revenue.
Those percentages cannot be summed into a 4% cash fee. The token allocation is
25 million whole tokens for a one-billion-token launch, paid only on migration.
Its value depends on price and market conditions. A lower token grant or a
vesting commitment would improve alignment; no such fee-token vesting is
implemented by the launchpad. The 50% Meme LP cut is a substantial reduction
in creator/reward/auto-LP yield; the 15% Quote cut is materially lighter.

Presale's 2% accepted raise plus 1% supply is easier to defend for an execution
service that charges nothing on failed/refunded sales. It still needs to be
quoted as two assets, alongside continuing 25% Meme / 15% Quote LP cuts (or the
actual legacy vault rates). Fairness also depends on the immutable creator split:
the holder Meme portion is treasury revenue, so the explicit 25% rate can
understate treasury receipts. A 50% holder share makes the treasury allocation
62.5% of gross Meme LP fees before a partner's cut of the explicit portion.

These are commercial judgments based on verified fee mechanics, not evidence
of demand elasticity, venue profitability or a market-wide pricing survey.
Changing economics is a separate decision; this task changes documentation only.

### Curve arithmetic and opening rounds

Let `q,m` be Quote/Meme fee fractions, and `b` the sell-burn fraction.
Buy: `quoteFee=ceil(g*q)`; `netQuote=g-quoteFee`;
`grossMeme=floor(M*netQuote/(V+Q+netQuote))`;
`memeFee=ceil(grossMeme*m)`; buyer gets `grossMeme-memeFee`.
Sell: `memeFee=ceil(inputMeme*m)`;
`burn=ceil((inputMeme-memeFee)*b)`;
`x=inputMeme-memeFee-burn`;
`grossQuote=floor((V+Q)*x/(M+x))`;
`quoteFee=ceil(grossQuote*q)`; seller gets the remainder.
[Full arithmetic](https://github.com/interest-protocol/blast-v2-contracts/blob/213d53cea237219a05fed3270f77e9429501216f/sui/launchpad/sources/curve.move#L35).

A capped buy consumes only
`g=ceil((target-Q)*10000/(10000-quoteFeeBps))`. The rest is returned
without a trade fee. Its net reaches the target **exactly**, because
`g-ceil(g*f/10000)=floor(g*(10000-f)/10000)=target-Q`.
A terminal buy can return zero Meme with minimum 0; nonterminal zero-output
ordinary buys abort. Tiny amounts can therefore suffer large effective
rates or fail due to ceiling rounding.
[Cap L14](https://github.com/interest-protocol/blast-v2-contracts/blob/213d53cea237219a05fed3270f77e9429501216f/sui/launchpad/sources/curve.move#L14),
[execution L819](https://github.com/interest-protocol/blast-v2-contracts/blob/213d53cea237219a05fed3270f77e9429501216f/sui/launchpad/sources/launch.move#L819),
[public buy L542](https://github.com/interest-protocol/blast-v2-contracts/blob/213d53cea237219a05fed3270f77e9429501216f/sui/launchpad/sources/launch.move#L542).

Opening-round bids pay no separate fee: the pot buys once using ordinary curve
fees and the target cap, then allocations/refunds are delivered pro rata with
remaining balances and bid totals; the last delivery receives remaining dust.
A nonterminal zero-output settlement keeps the entire pot for refunds.
[Opening-round flows L456](https://github.com/interest-protocol/blast-v2-contracts/blob/213d53cea237219a05fed3270f77e9429501216f/sui/launchpad/sources/launch.move#L456).

### Presale snapshots, amendments and refund arithmetic

Global `FeePolicy` changes only future sales. Admin may replace all four rates
for a pending sale, or an open sale strictly before `start_ms`. Changed raise
rate uses `floor(raise_target*rate/10000)` and moves against LP Quote; changed
token rate uses `floor(totalSupply*rate/10000)` and moves against buyer tokens.
Unchanged rates preserve previous exact apportioned amounts. Project recipient
amounts stay fixed. Positive LP Quote/buyer tokens and a seedable price must
remain. After the start, raise and token amounts are fixed; vault LP rates and
fee destinations remain governed.
[Amendment L680](https://github.com/interest-protocol/blast-v2-contracts/blob/213d53cea237219a05fed3270f77e9429501216f/sui/presale/sources/blast_presale.move#L680),
[unchanged-aware helpers L1354](https://github.com/interest-protocol/blast-v2-contracts/blob/213d53cea237219a05fed3270f77e9429501216f/sui/presale/sources/blast_presale.move#L1354).

With `T=totalCommitments`, `R=raiseTarget`, buyer allocation `A`, wallet
commitment `c`: successful claim receives `floor(A*c/T)` tokens and
`floor((T-R)*c/T)` Quote refund; cancellation returns `c`. Final claimant takes
all remaining balances, preserving funds. Protocol raise/token transfers are
part of the migration transaction, which aborts atomically if venue creation
fails. Funded but unmigrated sales can be cancelled at the liquidity deadline;
no successful-migration fee has then been paid.
[Claims L857](https://github.com/interest-protocol/blast-v2-contracts/blob/213d53cea237219a05fed3270f77e9429501216f/sui/presale/sources/blast_presale.move#L857),
[refund helpers L1546](https://github.com/interest-protocol/blast-v2-contracts/blob/213d53cea237219a05fed3270f77e9429501216f/sui/presale/sources/blast_presale.move#L1546).

## One-off vendor Aftermath presale

The separate `blast_aftermath_dao_presale` package creates a 50/50 Aftermath
pool with initial **2% DAO fee** on swaps, deposits and withdrawals, paid
directly to its compiled partner treasury, plus the **0.01% base swap-in fee**
compounding into locked LP. It creates no `presale_vault::Vault`: standard
Blast LP protocol cuts, holder-Meme routing and holder rounds do not apply.
Blast's core presale raise/token fees and optional partner share of the raise
fee still apply. The treasury receives Aftermath's `OwnerCap` and can change
the DAO fee and recipient through Aftermath; the package's initial 2% is not
a permanent pool price. This differs from the core Aftermath adapter, which
locks the owner cap in its vault and collects DAO-fee coins into the usual

## Partner cuts and fee recipients

`Partner.pay` takes `floor(BlastFee*currentPartnerShare/10000)` from a Blast
fee balance; Blast keeps the remainder. Share max 8,000 BPS (80%); Blast
changes wait seven days. Partner payout destination can change immediately
under its live cap. These cuts divide existing Blast revenue and add no user
levy. Products fix the chosen partner before launch/sale; later delisting
only stops future creation after notice.
[Partner pay L130](https://github.com/interest-protocol/blast-v2-contracts/blob/213d53cea237219a05fed3270f77e9429501216f/sui/partners/sources/blast_partners.move#L130),
[bounds L223](https://github.com/interest-protocol/blast-v2-contracts/blob/213d53cea237219a05fed3270f77e9429501216f/sui/partners/sources/blast_partners.move#L223).

Launchpad sharing covers accrued Blast SUI creation, Meme/Quote trade fees and
both migration allocations; its LP vault shares both explicit protocol LP
assets. Presale sharing covers the Quote raise fee and both explicit LP fee
assets, **not** the token-supply fee, holder Meme treasury routing, gas
reimbursement or creator-owned residuals. Partnered protocol balances wait
for permissionless pay/claim calls, applying current partner share and live
protocol destination then. Creator allocations and routed fees stay intact.
[Launch claim L678](https://github.com/interest-protocol/blast-v2-contracts/blob/213d53cea237219a05fed3270f77e9429501216f/sui/launchpad/sources/launch.move#L678),
[presale pay L947](https://github.com/interest-protocol/blast-v2-contracts/blob/213d53cea237219a05fed3270f77e9429501216f/sui/presale/sources/blast_presale.move#L947),
[presale LP pay L104](https://github.com/interest-protocol/blast-v2-contracts/blob/213d53cea237219a05fed3270f77e9429501216f/sui/presale/sources/presale_vault.move#L104).

## Publication defaults and validation limits

All initial **policy** fees are zero: launchpad creation and two trade rates,
protocol migration allocation, live curve-fee protocol share, migrator default
LP cuts, and all four presale protocol rates. Treasury destinations initially
use publisher address. These are not current commercial settings:
[launch config L236](https://github.com/interest-protocol/blast-v2-contracts/blob/213d53cea237219a05fed3270f77e9429501216f/sui/launchpad/sources/launch_config.move#L236),

The large source bounds (up to 100% LP revenue, nearly 100% allocation) are
validity limits, not fair-pricing guarantees. No timelock constrains changes
to curve-fee revenue split or LP protocol rates. Recipient allocation, creator
LP split, sell burn and Aftermath DAO fee are additional creator-selected
terms to inspect before participation. Existing snapshots are authoritative
for existing launch economics; global policy alone is insufficient.
